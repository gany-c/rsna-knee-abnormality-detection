## 2. Community labels, using the existing normalized images

On **September 28, 2026**, we trained DINOv2 with community-provided soft labels for the 4,349 studies without official labels, retaining the competition labels for the other 58. These label sources cover all **4,407 studies**, but the existing normalized images covered only **3,917**, so training used that subset.

Community targets were smoothed once using `p * 0.9 + 0.05` and assigned weight `0.25`. Official labels retained their binary values and weight `1.0`.

**DINOv2 Version 7: validation OOF AUROC 0.7356; public score 0.756.** The submission date is approximately September 28, based on the reported run history.

ResNet34 has now been retrained with these community labels; see the October 4 update below. The community-label ResNet achieved **0.831**, and the improved-model hybrid achieved **0.830**; see the submission findings below.

### Findings and decision

- ResNet improved from **0.679 to 0.831**, a gain of **0.152** over the earlier own-label submission. The improvement followed the community-label training update; this comparison does not isolate labels from every other training difference.
- ResNet exceeds the best recorded DINOv2 submission by **0.075**.
- The 80/20 hybrid scored **0.001 below ResNet** at the displayed precision. This blend did not demonstrate a public-score improvement; it does not prove that all blends would be worse or establish a statistically significant difference.
- **Retain ResNet34 submission Version 6 (0.831) as the baseline**, preserving its exact model package. Do not replace it with Hybrid Version 10.
- Next training experiment: resume the earlier epoch-11 run from its `last.pt`, rather than restarting with `RESUME_CHECKPOINT = None`. It was still improving when the configured time budget ended. Further gains are not guaranteed.

Public leaderboard results are development feedback, not a guarantee of final private-test ranking. The ResNet validation score remains based on only nine official studies.
